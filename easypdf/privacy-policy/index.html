Privacy Policy — EasyPDF

Effective date: October 25, 2026

DocumentsAdvertisingPrivacy choicesSharingStorageRetentionPermissionsChanges and contact

Documents

EasyPDF accesses files you choose using Android’s file picker or explicitly open or share with the app. Documents are processed locally on your device. The developer does not receive or upload your documents.

Advertising and third-party services

EasyPDF uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banner advertisements and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices. These services may collect and share IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, app/ad interaction information, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data handling depends on your consent, device settings, and Google’s service configuration. Network transmissions use encryption in transit. EasyPDF does not send the content of your documents to AdMob.

Privacy choices

Where required, a consent message is shown before advertising requests are permitted. Where available, open Settings → Ad privacy choices to review or change your choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. See Google’s privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and advertising information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Sharing

When you choose to share a document or open it in another application, Android passes the selected document to the app you choose. That app’s privacy policy applies.

Local storage and deletion

Reading preferences and advertising consent choices are stored on your device. Temporary document copies may be stored in the app’s private cache and deleted when the document is closed normally. Clear cache removes cached files left after an interruption. Clear storage or uninstall removes app preferences and cached files; your original documents are not deleted. File access grants may remain until revoked in Android settings, app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

Third-party retention

The developer has no document or account database for EasyPDF. Google handles data collected by its advertising services under its own retention and deletion practices; clearing EasyPDF’s local storage does not delete data already held by Google. See Google’s privacy policy for information and controls.

Permissions and accounts

EasyPDF does not require an account or broad storage access. Internet and network-state permissions support advertising and consent services. The advertising SDK may use the advertising ID when available and permitted.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when EasyPDF’s features or data practices change. For privacy questions or support, contact abirhasandipu.dev@gmail.com.
