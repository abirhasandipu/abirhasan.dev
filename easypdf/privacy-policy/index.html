Privacy Policy — EasyPDF

Effective date: October 25, 2026

EasyPDF is a document reader for Android. This policy explains how the current version handles your information.

DocumentsCollectionAdvertisingSharingStoragePermissionsChangesContact

Documents and file access

EasyPDF accesses files you choose using Android’s file picker or explicitly open or share with the app. Documents are processed locally on your device for viewing. EasyPDF does not upload your documents to the developer.

Information collection

The current version has no accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs. The developer does not collect or upload your documents, personal information, or device identifiers.

Advertising

The current version does not serve advertisements. A reserved banner area may appear in the interface, but it does not load ads or collect advertising data. If advertising is introduced, this policy will be updated to explain the advertising provider and its data practices.

Sharing and external applications

When you choose to share a document or open it in another application, Android passes the selected document to the application you choose. That application’s privacy policy applies to its handling of the document.

Local storage and retention

Reading preferences are stored on your device. Temporary document copies may be stored in EasyPDF’s private cache and are deleted when the document is closed normally. Cached files left after an interruption can be removed using Android’s Clear cache option.

Clear storage or uninstall EasyPDF to remove its local preferences and cached files. Your original documents are not deleted by these actions.

Permissions

The current version does not request broad storage access or internet access. Android may retain access grants for documents you select until you revoke them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app.

Changes

This policy may be updated when EasyPDF’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will reflect updates.
